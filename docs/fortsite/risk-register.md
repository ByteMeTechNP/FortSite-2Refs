# FortSite risk register

**Status:** Open planning risks. No mitigation is implemented, because every phase is NOT STARTED.  
**Phase:** 01. **NOT STARTED.**  
**Scoring:** This register does not invent probability or severity numbers. It states why the risk matters and which later phase is responsible for the control.

"Planned mitigation" means the phase that must build and test the control. It is not a control that exists today. Residual risk stays open until that phase is verified complete and the related acceptance scenario passes.

## Risks that are accepted as product boundaries

These are not scheduled away. Later phases must not quietly expand them.

- Push notification delivery can fail. The inbox is the record that counts.
- Automated takeoff, rebar schedules, and clash detection can be wrong. They stay draft until a qualified person acts, and clash status is not design approval.
- FortSite is not statutory payroll, not a government-certified billing product, and not a structural-design tool.
- Payment gateways, bank feeds, and e-sign providers are out of scope until a separate decision. Their absence is not a Phase 01 defect.
- Data residency is unknown until Phase 04. Operating as if a region had been chosen would be a false assurance.
